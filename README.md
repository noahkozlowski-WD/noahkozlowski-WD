Freelance web developer in South Africa. I build websites for businesses,
and the software that runs behind them.

I started making websites to pay for my first PC. Later I co-founded

Most of that work sits in private repositories. The write-ups live on

## Selected work, 2026

**[ADvantage](https://noahkozlowski.com/#projects)** &nbsp; <sub>SaaS, ad intelligence</sub><br>
Pulls every ad a niche is running from Meta's Ad Library, including video, carousels and copy,
then scores each one with AI. I did the product design, the scraping engine and the full stack.<br>
<sub>Next.js, PostgreSQL, Playwright, Gemini</sub>

**[BetterLeads](https://noahkozlowski.com/#projects)** &nbsp; <sub>Sales automation</sub><br>
Finds local businesses on Google Maps for a growth consultancy, qualifies them with AI against
a rubric the team can edit, and sends personalised email campaigns across rotating mailboxes.<br>
<sub>Next.js, Prisma, PostgreSQL, IMAP/SMTP</sub>

**[Better Growth Company](https://noahkozlowski.com/#projects)** &nbsp; <sub>Marketing sites</sub><br>
Five connected sites for the same consultancy, built on one design system: the brand site,
a lead magnet, a sales page, offerings and advisory.<br>
<sub>Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Resend</sub>

**[Mew Duo Dash](https://noahkozlowski.com/#projects)** &nbsp; <sub>Tournament platform</sub><br>
Runs a community 2v2 Valorant event in South Africa. Registration, rank-balanced team matching,
brackets that advance on their own, and an admin dashboard.<br>
<sub>React, FastAPI, MongoDB</sub>
